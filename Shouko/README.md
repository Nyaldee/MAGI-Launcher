Shouko is an all-round Discord bot built to bring a server to life: a full economy with its own mini-games, a creature-raising and collecting game, a pile of fun commands, complete moderation tools, and a few everyday utilities. Here is everything it can do once invited to a server.

> Shouko speaks French and English: an administrator picks the server's language with `/install config langue`, and slash commands are shown in each member's Discord language.

## 💰 Economy

- `/eco` — wallet and bank, daily/weekly/monthly rewards played as short adventures (Attack/Negotiate/Flee choices, fishing, exploration...), a leaderboard of the richest members, giving, duelling or asking other members for coins, bank deposits and withdrawals, and a whole game room: higher-or-lower, a treasure hunt where you pick doors, a slot machine, blackjack against the bot, or simply throwing your coins to whoever clicks first.
- The built-in shop sells cosmetic roles set up by the staff, cooldown resets, treasure chests, and quick fishing trips for a few coins.
- Staff can start group mini-games on demand or run a custom quiz with a reward to liven up a channel.
- When an economy channel is set, small surprise events pop up on their own (guess the Moemon/Pokémon/Genshin character, a lost wallet to pick up, chests, a maths question, a quiz, a pairs game, a typing race...) — first come, first served.

## 🐾 Moemon — raise your creature

- `/moehub` — adopt a Moemon that hatches from an egg, feed her, play with her, keep an eye on her hunger, mood, cleanliness and health, evolve her, change her nature, leave her at the daycare to gain XP passively, or bring her back to life if she wastes away from neglect.
- Challenge another player's Moemon to a duel (Fire/Water/Leaf, rock-paper-scissors style), trade Moemon or their items, or pair two Moemon as a couple for a daily XP bonus.
- `/moedex` — a Pokédex-style collection: go on a daily photo trip to catch random new species, browse your Moédex, compare yourself with others on the leaderboard, or trade a specific species with another player.

## 🎉 Fun

- Duels and games between members: Rock-Paper-Scissors, 4×4 tic-tac-toe with animated emojis, a typing race solo or with others.
- Entertainment: jokes with a delayed punchline, a themed magic 8-ball (Genshin, Final Fantasy, Xenoblade), kaomoji, Japanese omikuji fortune draws, themed conversation starters, deliberately endless light-novel titles, Pokémon fusions, random Genshin character draws, and a player that serves up music, openings and soundtracks at the click of a button.
- Animated GIF interactions between members (hug, pat, kiss...), fake Russian roulettes and bets with a timeout as the penalty, a love/compatibility meter between two members, auto-generated themed nicknames (Halloween, Christmas, synthwave...), custom fake Minecraft advancement toasts, mood reactions in GIF form, and an open quiz where a wrong answer earns a small comedic timeout.

## ℹ️ Info

- `/info` — a member's avatar, banner and full profile, the list of boosters and invites, server statistics, bot statistics, or every member of a given role.
- `/genshin` — a Genshin Impact player card (level, achievements, Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater, Stygian Onslaught, character showcase) from a simple UID.
- `/jikan` — a MyAnimeList explorer: search, detailed pages, the current season, the weekly schedule, random anime/manga/character picks.
- `/minecraftplayer` — a preview of a player's Minecraft skin.

## 🛡️ Moderation

- Complete sanctions: warnings, timeouts (including by public vote), kicks, bans and unbans, with confirmations and safeguards at every step.
- Server lockdown à la carte: channel, reactions, uploads, invites, @everyone mentions, external apps, anti-link/anti-spam protection, webhook purge.
- Event tools: point-based teams for a contest, polls and dilemmas, a draw among a message's reactions, a random member spotlight, message purges, a palette of coloured roles generated in one click.
- Server setup: welcome/logs/contact channels, roles for sale in the economy shop, a "join to create" channel for temporary voice channels.
- The bot can also speak for the staff: a plain message, a rich message, a scheduled message, a DM, or a standalone link button.

## 🛠️ Utility

- `/calculate` — an advanced calculator (trigonometry, logarithms, factorials, roots...).
- `/translate` — translation between 25 languages.
- `/weather` — live weather for a city.

## Behind the scenes

- A "Join to Create" voice channel can be set up: joining it instantly creates a personal voice channel with management rights for its owner, deleted automatically once empty.
- A personalised welcome message for every new member.
- An automatic announcement when someone boosts the server.
- Anti-spam / anti-link filtering with automatic sanctions in protected channels.
- `/help` brings all of this together in an interactive menu organised by category.
